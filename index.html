Loginforce for Salesforce

A browser extension for consultants and admins who work in many Salesforce orgs a day: saved org logins in one click, log in as another user in this tab, a new tab or incognito, and a colour for every org.

  • Everything stays in your browser. Saved passwords are encrypted under a master password you choose.
  • No server, no analytics, no tracking.
